Zinra

Privacy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Zinra records the Chrome tab you choose, then edits and exports that recording on your computer. It is built to work offline after capture.

What Zinra accesses

  • Tab capture — only the tab you start recording, plus optional tab audio.
  • Pointer position — sampled on that tab while a recording is armed, so zooms can follow clicks. Sampling stops when you stop.
  • Downloads — to save the exported MP4 where you pick.
  • Sync storage — your preferences (zoom depth, quality, audio). No recordings are uploaded.

What Zinra does not do

  • No account. No analytics SDK. No advertising ID.
  • Recordings are not sent to Zinra servers. Export runs in the extension window with WebCodecs.
  • Zinra does not read other tabs in the background. Capture and pointer sampling run only while you record.

Permissions

tabCapture, tabs, activeTab, scripting, storage, and downloads exist so Zinra can record a tab, place zooms, remember settings, and save a file. Host access is used only to inject the recorder helper on the tab you film.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Chrome Web Store support email listed on the Zinra listing, or open an issue on GitHub.
