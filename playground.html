How auto punch-in decides where to zoom
Runs when: you recorded this page (not the entire screen) with "Mark a zoom on each click" on. Clicks and typing zoom to 1.50×; clicks within ~1.25s and close together merge into one clip, far-apart clicks each get their own, typing zooms stay on the field.

Doesn't run when: you shared the entire screen, turned the checkbox off, or clicked outside Chrome — you still get a recording, just add Zoom clips by hand on the timeline.
