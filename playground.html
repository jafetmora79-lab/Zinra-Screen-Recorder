Try the punch-in zoom here

This page exists for one reason: something to click on while you test Zinra, without needing a real site or worrying about anything you record. Nothing here is tracked or sent anywhere.

1 Open Zinra

Click the Zinra icon in your toolbar, then Record this page.

2 Click a few tiles

Try clicking the same tile twice, then try different tiles far apart.

3 Stop & edit

Stop from the toolbar or the recorder tab, then look at the Zoom track.

How the auto punch-in decides where to zoom: clicks within about 3 seconds of each other, close together on the page, get grouped into a single zoom clip centered on them. Clicks far apart in time or position each get their own clip. Typing into a field gets a clip too, framed around the field itself.

Six things to click

Each one reacts differently, so you can see the zoom land on the actual spot you clicked - not just a generic box.

Nothing on this page reads, stores, or sends anything - it's local DOM state that resets on reload. Back to zinra.
