How it works

Four steps, one continuous take.

00:00

Hit record

Start the tab you're already on. Zinra stays out of the way while you work — no borders, no toolbar to explain to whoever's watching the take.

00:08

It punches in for you

Every click gets a zoom, sized and timed automatically at a standard 1.50×. Typing gets one too. You never touch a keyframe by hand.

00:24

Cut it on a real timeline

Zoom, speed, cut, and trim — plus a voiceover or music track, an aspect-ratio crop for phone screens, and a synthetic cursor for tab recordings that never had one to begin with.

01:10

Export, offline

Zinra seeks through the take frame by frame and encodes an MP4 locally with WebCodecs. It never re-plays the clip in real time to re-record it, and it never leaves your machine.

What's in the timeline

Everything a screen recording actually needs.

Auto zoom on clicks

Standard 1.50× depth, tune it from shallow to maximum, and hold time is automatic.

Speed ramps

Slow-mo down to 0.25× or fast-forward up to 8× on any section of the take.

Clean cuts

Drop dead air entirely without ever having to re-record the tab.

Voiceover & music

Drop in an MP3 or WAV as its own track — mixed into the export, glued to the moment you place it.

Synthetic cursor

Tab recordings have no visible pointer by default. Draw one back in — Arrow and Dot are free.

HAND & RING · PRO

Aspect-ratio crop

16:9, 9:16 for phone screens, 1:1, or 4:5 — cropped straight from full resolution, never rescaled first.
